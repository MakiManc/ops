01 · Method

Why payday weeks had to come out

UK payday is the last working day of the month. A week that contains one — or follows one — trades on money that just landed, not on whether the restaurant is any good. Fourteen of the thirty weeks are payday-inflated; sixteen are not. Growth in the lean weeks is the honest read on whether a GM is building a base through service and standards. Every figure below is also ranked against the fleet median for the same weeks, so a July uplift that every site got for seasonal reasons earns nobody any credit.

05 · The thing that isn't a person

Eleven of sixteen sites are shrinking. You can exit four managers and still be a smaller business in October, because the erosion is estate-wide and started before July. The median site lost 6.6% of its lean-week revenue between Q1 and July. If that is a pricing, marketing or menu problem, no GM change fixes it — and the freshers campaign in September is landing on a base that is already contracting.
Cost discipline splits from growth. Imre Balogh at Soho is the second-best base-builder in the company (+17.0% covers, and the least payday-dependent site at +0.8%) while running a 36.7% wage in lean weeks. He is buying the growth. That is a coachable problem, not a capability one — but it needs naming before it becomes a habit.
