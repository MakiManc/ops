02 · How the composite is built

Four dimensions, weights unchanged from Scorecard v1

This extends the existing AM_GM_Scorecard_v1 — same four dimensions, same weights, same 1–5 scale. It is one system, not a parallel one. The detailed review still lives in the GM Review Template and Calculator in the GM/AGM Performance Reviews Drive folder.

35%
Financial
Sales Performance · Cost Management. Both computed from live data — five-week means of sales vs target, wage % and food %.
25%
People
Training Standards (reviewer) · Retention W/R, recomputed from Staff Out ÷ mean headcount.
20%
Standards
Customer Satisfaction (70% review + 30% site Google weekly average) · Hygiene/Compliance · Operational Efficiency.
20%
Capability
Aptitude Set · Admin Efficiency · Marketing. All three are reviewer ratings.

Conversion rules

Edit these constants and the whole workbook re-bands

The yellow cells in the workbook. They are reproduced here so a number on this page can always be traced back to the band that produced it.

Sales 1–5MAX(1, MIN(5, 3 + vs_target% / X))X = 7.50% vs target = 3.0 · +15% = 5 · −15% = 1
Wage 1–5MAX(1, MIN(5, 5 − (wage% − A) / B))A = 1919% = 5 · 23% = 3 · 27% = 1
B = 2
Food 1–5MAX(1, MIN(5, 5 − (food% − C) / D))C = 2121% = 5 · 25% (target ceiling) = 3 · 29% = 1
D = 2
Google 1–5MAX(1, MIN(5, 5 − (E − rating) × F))E = 4.94.9★ = 5 · 4.7★ (AMO1 KR) = 4 · 4.5★ = 3
F = 5
Google ★ for M10 is 4.86 (latest week). The 5-week mean of 5.24 is impossible on a 5-star scale — the w/c 29/06 pull returned 6.33.

Method

What this is, and what it is not

A quarterly tracker for every FOH site manager — GM, RM, AGM, DAM. It scores each manager 1–5 on four weighted dimensions and rolls them into one composite. The detailed review is unchanged and still lives in the GM Review Template and Calculator in the GM/AGM Performance Reviews Drive folder. This page reads that output alongside live operating data; it does not replace it.

What changed in v2

Financial is no longer a rating. Sales Performance and Cost Management are computed from live site data — five-week means, w/c 29/06–27/07/2026, from the AM Control Centre — not from an AM’s subjective 1–5. Retention comes from the Staff Retention sheet. Customer Satisfaction blends the review rating 70% with the site’s Google weekly average 30%. The other six measures remain reviewer ratings.

How to refresh each quarter

  1. Paste the new sub-scores
    Export the quarter’s Google Form responses and paste the per-manager sub-scores into the Q3 Input tab of the workbook.
  2. Update the two live inputs
    Refresh the five-week means on Site KPIs and the leaver counts on Retention. Those are the only yellow cells that matter.
  3. Let it recalculate
    Everything on GM Scorecard and Exit Risk recomputes. Do not overwrite the formula columns. Then rebuild this page from the workbook.
