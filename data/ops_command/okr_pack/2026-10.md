How the scorecard is scored: one row per KR on the 2026 Operations Input sheet - six objectives, thirty KRs, in the sheet's own order and wording. Every KR scores 100, 80, 50 or 0 from the band table in the builder (scorecard.bands) - or, for OO2 KR2 alone, by the one fixed rule beside it (scorecard.band_rules: a month with 0 repeat issues scores 100, Ross 15/10/2026) - and RAG follows the score: 100 green, 80 or 50 amber, 0 red. A band still marked PROPOSED scores exactly like an agreed one and says so on the row until Matthew confirms it. A KR with no target gets no score, and a KR with no source is grey with its blocker named - that list is the remaining build. An objective's percentage is the mean of the KRs that HAVE a score that month; an unscored KR is left out of the mean and never counted as a zero, which is why each objective also says how many of its five scored. The Operations percentage is the mean of OO2-OO6; OO1 is Finance's and is excluded. Five KRs are typed into the sheet itself and read from it: OO2 KR3, OO3 KR3 and OO4 KR1 from their log tabs, OO4 KR4 from Finance's % actioned, and OO6 KR1 from Matthew's supply chain availability %; OO6 KR2 also reads the sheet's monthly 'all POs invoiced' cell beside JFC's POs. A month left blank on the sheet is not scored. OO6 (Frozen Ramen) is measured from October 2026: KR3 from Ross's weekly stock count report; KR4 and KR5 have no agreed measure and stay grey. OO1 KR1-KR5 are read from Finance's management-account summary on the 7th of each month and scored PASS/FAIL on each KR's own target (Ross, 15/10/2026) - SCORES ONLY: no figure behind an OO1 score is published, and OO1 shows no objective percentage. OO2 KR5 is read from the Maintenance Contact List 2026. The Operating KPIs below the scorecard are measurements that are not on Matthew's sheet - they are real and Ross uses them, they are simply not what he is scored on.

How months are scored: the month picker moves the rows whose KR is a per-month measure. A month still in progress is SCORED only once it holds 7 or more days of the KR's own source data (Ross, 09/10/2026); before that its value is shown with 'MTD', score and RAG are left empty, and the objective percentage leaves the row out. A KR that COUNTS (issues, spikes, closures, disruptions, damaged items) is different, because a count can only rise: it scores 0 as soon as it is already past its last tolerance, and otherwise stays MTD until the month closes - it is never green early. A closed month is always scored. Every month it offers was scored HERE, in the builder, against the same band and the same rule as the default month - the picker chooses between finished answers and computes nothing. A KR with no monthly form does not follow it, and counts towards the default month's objective percentage only: carrying a current-state figure back into an earlier month would invent a history the measurement does not have.
