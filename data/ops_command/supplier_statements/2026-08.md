Basis: Every delivery/supplier issue form in GetCompliant whose own answer date falls in August 2026 (2026-08-01 to 2026-08-31), deduped by FormId - the same rows the Supplier Issues tab and OO3 KR1 count - grouped by the supplier answered on the form (or found in its text when the question was left blank). Issue text is VERBATIM. Category is a keyword heuristic over that text, not a taxonomy. A row is credit/invoice-flagged when its text mentions a credit, invoice, refund, rebate or charge, or its category is invoice_credit. The issue-free rate is supply.otif's for the month and is NOT on-time-in-full: ISSUE-FREE DELIVERY RATE (INDICATIVE) - this is NOT OTIF and must not be labelled as one: nothing here observes whether a delivery was on time, and one delivery can carry several issue forms, so the rate can only ever be a lower bound. deliveries = orders with a delivery date in the month from the Kobas Order Emails feed (deduped by Kobas Reference); issues = supplier issues from GC Form Task Answers whose own answer date falls in the month, deduped by FormId - the identical rows the Supplier Issues tab and the KR1 gauge read, so all three reconcile; deliveries are counted only up to the newest day the issue feed covers (2026-10-10) - one dated later cannot have had an issue filed yet, so it is held back (deliveries_held_back) rather than counted as issue-free, and one dated on a day no pull of the issue feed covers is left out (deliveries_in_feed_holes) for the same reason; rate = max(0, deliveries - issues) / deliveries, and is published ONLY for a supplier-month whose order emails cover the whole month for that supplier. Every other supplier-month carries not_measured saying why, and is excluded from the all-supplier total. Real OTIF needs a source that records what was DELIVERED against what was ORDERED - Mapal Supplier Orders, or a delivery file from Lynas.
