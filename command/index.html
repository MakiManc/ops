Two different measurements sit on this page. The factory cards are the factory's own refractometer readings of the batch it produced — the score is the reading taken after ice is added, graded against its product's spec (tonkotsu 8–9, chicken 5–6). The site cards below are GetCompliant broth checks of broth as served, graded against their own site spec (chicken 6±1, tonkotsu 6–7). Same instrument, different moment in the broth's life — which is why the two sets of numbers are different and neither band transfers to the other. They are shown separately and never averaged into one another.
