Add / Remove — live decisions

Decides itself · checking freshness… override in sheet ↗
How the decision is made: a KILL or SCALE flag must hold for at least 3 full days (and 3 consecutive runs) before it is confirmed automatically — KILL becomes REMOVE, SCALE becomes ADD. KEEP settles on sight. A campaign has to be genuinely bad, or genuinely good, for three days running — one odd morning proves nothing, and re-running the job twice in a day buys no time. Nothing is paused or spent automatically: the matrix makes the call, a human actions it in the ad account.
To disagree, type ADD / REMOVE / KEEP into manual_confirm in the sheet — your value wins and the row shows as overridden. You no longer need to confirm anything for the system to move.
Organic rule: KILL if median eng/reach <2% OR saves+shares/reach <0.5% (last 28d, ≥3 posts). SCALE if median eng/reach ≥6% OR any post ≥3× rolling median reach.
Paid rule (CPA, approved 7 Jul): SCALE if CPA ≤£5 with 5+ conversions · KILL if CPA >£12 after £50+ spend, or zero conversions after £75+ (last 30d). Awareness/boost campaigns keep the CTR/CPC rule (KILL CTR<0.8% + CPC>£1.50 · SCALE CTR>1.5% + CPC<£0.60).
