Add / Remove — live decisions

Decides itself · checking freshness… override in sheet ↗
The four numbers, in plain English — and who fixes what:
£/RESULT — what one booking, purchase or lead costs us. £5 or under = scale it up · over £12 = kill it. Owner: Vidya (paid media).
£/CLICK — what one click costs. Expensive clicks mean the wrong audience or bidding. Owner: Vidya (paid media).
LPV % — of the people who clicked, how many actually saw the page load. Red under 70% = the landing page is slow or broken — fix the page before judging the ad. Owner: Vidya (landing pages & tracking).
THRUPLAY % — of the people who started the video, how many watched it through. Red under 60% = creative problem — the video loses people, so change the video, not the budget. Owner: Georgie (creative).
